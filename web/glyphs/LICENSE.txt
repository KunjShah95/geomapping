Map label glyphs (MapLibre/Mapbox SDF PBF ranges) for the AUDA DP 2021 viewer.

Fontstacks: "Noto Sans Regular", "Noto Sans Bold" - all 256 ranges (0-255 .. 65280-65535),
including Devanagari (2304-2559) and Gujarati (2560-2815) for the planned label toggle.

Source: MapLibre demo tiles glyph set, https://demotiles.maplibre.org/font/{fontstack}/{range}.pbf
        (repository https://github.com/maplibre/demotiles, BSD-3-Clause)
